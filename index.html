1Why Sell Greece?

Greece is one of Europe's most enchanting destinations, where ancient history meets sun-soaked islands and a culture that celebrates food, family, and fun. From the Acropolis in Athens to the iconic sunsets of Santorini and the nightlife of Mykonos, every traveller finds their version of paradise here.

Top Reasons to Visit

  • Breathtaking Islands: Each with its own charm, Santorini for romance, Mykonos for nightlife, Naxos for family-friendly beaches, and Corfu for lush landscapes.
  • Incredible History: Athens is an open-air museum, where Western civilisation was born.
  • The Food: Think feta, olives, fresh seafood, moussaka, souvlaki, gyros, spanakopita, and baklava. Every meal feels like a celebration and every ingredient is fresh.
  • Natural Beauty: Volcanic landscapes, dramatic cliffs, and villages perched above the Aegean.
  • Hospitality & Value: Warm locals and great value for money compared to Western Europe.
  • Sunshine: Over 250 days of sunshine a year, summer starts early and ends late.

3Highlights of Athens

  • Acropolis & Museum Tour: Visit the Panathenaic Stadium, Temple of Olympian Zeus, and Acropolis Museum. This is a must do, no trip to Athens is complete without a visit to the Acropolis.
  • Athens Food Walking Tour: Sample local delicacies, olives, cheese, honey, and Greek meze.
  • Day Cruise to Hydra, Aegina & Poros: Ideal for a taste of island life without leaving the mainland.
  • Catamaran Sunset Cruise: Sail the Athenian coast with a drink in hand as the sun sets.
  • Plaka (Old City): Located at the base of the Acropolis, the Plaka area is like taking a step back in time. Walk cobbled streets, browse souvenir shops, or enjoy an impromptu meze at a taverna, a glimpse into the Athens that used to be.

4Getting Around

In Athens

  • Metro: Clean, safe, and efficient, connects major attractions. Metro stations are mini museums, showcasing ancient artifacts discovered while the metro was being developed.
  • Walking: Many historic sites are within walking distance of Plaka and Syntagma.
  • Taxis / Apps: Taxis are plentiful; use apps like Beat for reliability.
  • Hop-On Hop-Off Bus: Great for a quick orientation of the city.

Between the Islands

  • Ferries: The classic way to island-hop. The main port, Piraeus, has frequent ferries to most islands. Example routes: Athens to Mykonos ~4-5 hrs (fast ferry); Athens to Santorini ~5-8 hrs; Mykonos to Paros ~1 hr. Ferries offer both economy and business class. Self-drive clients can take their car onto the ferry.
  • Flights: Quick and convenient for longer hops (e.g. Athens to Crete or Rhodes). Recommendation: fly between Athens and Mykonos and between Athens and Santorini, as ferry trips, even high-speed ones, can eat into holiday time. Use ferry travel between the islands of the Cyclades (Mykonos, Naxos, Paros and Santorini).
  • Private Transfers: For luxury clients, private catamarans and small-group charters are increasingly popular.

On the Islands

How you explore the island is part of the adventure. Options for getting around:

  • Scooters: Very popular with tourists, the perfect way to explore hidden gems. Travellers must have an international driver's licence to hire a scooter.
  • Car Hire: Guests can hire a car for the time they're on the island, but roads tend to be narrow and parking at the property might be challenging.
  • Local Buses: Reliable services connect main towns to beaches and villages. Affordable, operate frequently during summer months.
  • Taxis: Less popular, limited and wait times can be long on the popular islands.
  • Water Taxis: Common in Mykonos, Paros and Crete, connecting popular beaches. Fun, scenic, and avoids the winding roads.
  • Mini Tourist Trains: Popular family-orientated islands like Naxos, Paros, Corfu, Kos and Crete offer these, running short routes between beaches, harbours and town centres.
  • Bicycles or e-Bikes: A great eco-friendly and fun way to explore.
  • Cable Car or Funicular (Santorini): Used to get from the port to Fira Town at the top of the cliff. Avoid the donkey paths and don't ride the donkeys.

5Island Highlights

A typical night on the islands only starts at about 10pm when restaurants become busy and vibey with patrons. Before dinner, visitors can enjoy happy hour specials in bars, enjoying the views. Nightclubs only open at 11pm or midnight and stay open until 5/6am. Street food vendors operate throughout the night. Because of the late nights, shops and tavernas usually only open at around 10am.

The Cyclades Islands

Mykonos
  • Known as the party capital with chic beach clubs and bars at Little Venice, offering happy hour specials while enjoying the views of the Mediterranean.
  • Beautiful southern beaches.
  • Ideal for younger adults or couples wanting style and nightlife.
Santorini
  • The romantic showstopper with its caldera views, whitewashed villages, and famous sunsets in Oia.
  • Not known for beaches (volcanic black or red sand and very narrow).
  • Best for honeymooners, photographers, and luxury travellers.
Paros
  • Family-oriented and less crowded.
  • Lovely villages like Naoussa and Lefkes, best explored by car.
  • Great for travellers seeking authenticity and calm.
Naxos
  • The largest Cycladic island with beaches, ruins, and mountain villages.
  • Excellent for families and nature lovers.

The Dodecanese Islands

Rhodes
  • The largest of the Dodecanese Islands.
  • Located just off the west coast of Turkey.
  • A blend of culture and beach life, home to the medieval Old Town (UNESCO-listed).
  • Great nightlife and some of the sunniest weather in Europe.
Kos
  • 3rd largest island in the Dodecanese.
  • Home of Hippocrates, the Father of Medicine.

The Ionian Islands

Corfu
  • Green and lush with Italian influences due to its close proximity to Italy.
  • Easy flight from Athens; ideal for older travellers or couples.

The Sporades Islands

Skiathos
  • 60 sandy beaches on 44km of coastline.
  • Bleached white cliffs backed by pine forests.
  • Inspired the film Mamma Mia.
  • Cosmopolitan nightlife.
Skopelos
  • Mountains, pine forests, clear seas and wonderful beaches attract a more discerning traveller.
  • Steeped in history; historic monasteries can be discovered in the mountains and forests.

Crete

Crete
  • The biggest island, full of culture, history, and beaches.
  • Perfect for longer stays.
  • Fly into Chania or Heraklion; best explored by car.

7Food & Street Eats

Greek cuisine is all about freshness, simplicity, and flavour. Encourage clients to eat like locals.

Must-Try Dishes

  • Souvlaki & Gyros (Yiros): grilled meat skewers or wraps with pita and tzatziki. Gyros are the best street food, and even have French fries inside.
  • Moussaka: baked layers of aubergine, minced meat, and béchamel.
  • Spanakopita: spinach and feta pastry.
  • Dolmades: vine leaves stuffed with rice and herbs.
  • Fresh Seafood: octopus, sardines, and calamari by the sea, tavernas get their octopus straight from the sea and grill it on braais/BBQs outside the restaurant.
  • Baklava: flaky pastry layered with nuts and honey.
  • Loukoumades: Greek doughnuts with honey and cinnamon, a street food staple.

10Sales Tip for Consultants

When selling Greece:

  • Tailor by personality, not just budget.
  • Combine two to three islands for variety, e.g. Athens + Mykonos + Santorini or Athens + Paros + Naxos. Don't squeeze too many islands into one itinerary or clients will spend most of their time travelling between islands instead of immersing themselves.
  • Emphasise experiences, sunset sailing, local cooking classes, or island road trips. Greece is about culture, exploring, and a slower pace of life: stopping at a hidden taverna and enjoying a fresh meal with a cold drink. This is Greece.
  • Beaches in Greece are not amazing: travellers imagine Mauritius, lush and green with swaying palm trees, but the islands themselves are quite stark and rocky. Some islands do have lovely beaches, but they're narrow and quite small. Some are pebbly, and Santorini has black or red sand beaches, as it's a volcanic island.
  • Book early for July-August travel, especially in Santorini and Mykonos.
